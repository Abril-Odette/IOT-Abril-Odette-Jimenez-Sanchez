About Them


- - - - Short Biography (Early Days & Evolution)- - - -

High School Roots & Band Origins:

The origins of the band trace back to the early 1990s when Julian Casablancas and Nikolai Fraiture met as childhood friends in New York. Later, while attending the Le Rosey boarding school in Switzerland, Casablancas met Albert Hammond Jr. Years later, after serendipitously meeting again in Manhattan while studying, Hammond Jr. moved into Casablancas’ apartment, and the two began writing songs that defined the band's initial catalog.

The Transgressor / Music Factory Era:

Before adopting the name The Strokes, the group practiced relentlessly in a small, windowless basement rehearsal space in New York's Lower East Side. They played their very first public gig at the iconic venue Spiral in Manhattan in 1999, honing a tight, hyper-rehearsed live set before catching the attention of local talent scouts.





- - - - Fast Facts (Did You Know?) - - - -

The Producer Behind the Sound:

Their early breakthrough was heavily shaped by producer Gordon Raphael, who recorded their debut EP The Modern Age using raw, analog methods to preserve their live room energy.

Grammy Recognition:

Despite being one of the defining bands of the 2000s, they received their very first Grammy Award twenty years after their debut, winning Best Rock Album in 2021 for The New Abnormal.

Side Projects & Ventures:

Every member of the band has explored independent creative projects. Julian Casablancas founded the independent record label Cult Records and fronted the experimental band The Voidz, while Albert Hammond Jr. has released several successful solo albums.



- - - - Career Timeline Highlights - - - -

1999 - 2000: Wrote and refined their early repertoire in Manhattan basements, releasing The Modern Age EP which sparked an unprecedented record label bidding war.

2006 - 2010: Entered an extended hiatus following the tour for First Impressions of Earth, allowing members to pursue personal projects and regroup with fresh creative perspectives.

2011 - 2013: Experimented with new musical directions on Angles (2011) and Comedown Machine (2013), shifting toward synth-heavy textures and 80s new wave arrangements.

2020 - Present: Partnered with legendary producer Rick Rubin at his Shangri-La studio in Malibu to record The New Abnormal, marking a major artistic resurgence.
