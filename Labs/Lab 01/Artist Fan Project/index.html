The Strokes Page

The Strokes are an acclaimed American rock band formed in New York City in 1998. Consisting of Julian Casablancas (lead vocals), Nick Valensi (lead guitar), Albert Hammond Jr. (rhythm guitar), Nikolai Fraiture (bass), and Fabrizio Moretti (drums), the band spearheaded the early-2000s garage rock revival and became a defining voice of 21st-century indie rock.



- - - - Information - - - -
  • Members: Julian Casablancas (lead vocals), Nick Valensi (lead guitar), Albert Hammond Jr. (rhythm guitar), Nikolai Fraiture (bass), and Fabrizio Moretti (drums).
  • Formed: 1998 in New York City.
  • Debut Album: Is This It (2001) – universally acclaimed as a masterpiece of modern rock.
  • Signature Sound: Twin-guitar harmonies, driving basslines, energetic drum beats, and relaxed, crooning vocals.
  • Cultural Impact: Credited with reviving rock and roll in the early 2000s and influencing bands like Arctic Monkeys, Franz Ferdinand, and Kings of Leon.
